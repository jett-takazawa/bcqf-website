# Boston College Quantitative Finance Club

Building Boston College's presence in quantitative trading, research, and development.

---

## About Us

Founded in 2026, the Boston College Quantitative Finance Club is a group of BC students preparing for careers in quantitative trading, research, and development.

We're here to share what we know with each other and with the wider BC community, and to make Boston College a recognized name in quantitative finance.

---

## What We Do

### 01 — Education

Throughout the year, we run internal seminars on probability, market microstructure, quantitative strategies, and other core topics in quantitative finance. No prior trading experience is required.

### 02 — Industry Connections

We're building relationships with leading firms in the industry to give members access to mentorship, networking, and firsthand insight into quant careers.

### 03 — Community

We're a small group of about 25 undergraduates with a flat structure, so every member plays a real part in shaping the club. We're planning social events throughout the year, including coffee chats, dinners, and retreats.
